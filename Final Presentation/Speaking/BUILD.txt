Build the speaking PDF from this folder:
  powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\build.ps1

Automatically rebuild whenever the LaTeX source changes:
  powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\build.ps1 -Watch
Stop watching with Ctrl+C. Keep the terminal open while editing.
